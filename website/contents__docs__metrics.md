### basics.mdx

Markdown page “Why you need metrics”. Metrics are numbers about your system measured over
time: how many requests you served, how long they took, how deep the job queue is, how much
memory a process is using. Where [logs](/docs/logs) record individual events and
[traces](/docs/distributed-tracing) follow individual requests, metrics summarize
everything. They answer "how is the system doing?" at a glance, cheaply, all the time.
