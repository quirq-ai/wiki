### bodies.js

Astronomical data. Planet orbital elements: JPL "Keplerian Elements for Approximate
Positions of the Major Planets" (E.M. Standish), valid 1800 AD to 2050 AD. Each entry is
[value at J2000.0, rate per Julian century]; a in AU, angles in degrees. w is longitude of
perihelion (varpi), node is longitude of ascending node.
